Faces of integrity

Black African people modelling integrity, honesty and trust.

The African Center for Governance, Asset Recovery and Sustainable Development is an independent civil society organisation based in Nigeria. We work at the intersection of governance, anti-corruption, international cooperation and development.

01 · Integrity

Principles you can see in action.

Integrity grows when people honour commitments, act transparently and keep the public interest at the centre.

02 · Trust

Trust is built through open dialogue.

Respectful collaboration, listening and shared responsibility strengthen institutions and communities.

03 · Honesty

Honesty creates public confidence.

Clear communication and truthful leadership help people understand decisions, duties and results.

04 · Accountability

Leadership that answers with integrity.

Accountability means welcoming scrutiny, explaining choices and using power responsibly for development.
